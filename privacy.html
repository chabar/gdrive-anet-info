Privacy Policy for gdrive_anet

Effective date: 27 September 2026

About this utility

gdrive_anet is a private utility used by the anet personal home platform operator. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive data and use

When authorized, the utility receives broad permission to access and manage files in the Google Drive account. Its automated tasks use that access to synchronize designated anet data (home sensors), logs, and analysis files between Google Drive and anet servers.

Storage and sharing

Files may be stored in Google Drive and copied to anet servers for synchronization, analysis, and operation of the platform. The utility does not sell file contents or use them for advertising. Google and the hosting provider process data as needed to provide Drive and server infrastructure.

Access and deletion

The platform operator controls the Google account and the systems that use this utility. Revoking its Google access stops future synchronization, but does not automatically delete copies already stored on anet servers. For questions or deletion requests, contact charles.barthelemy@gmail.com.
